Privacy Policy

Jenns Event Calendar, an events and RSVP app for Root communities.

Last updated September 24, 2026

The Short Version

The app saves the events your community makes and who said they're going. Your RSVPs are visible to the rest of the community, and they're deleted 90 days after the event. Nothing leaves Root, and nothing is sold or used for ads.

What It Saves

  • Events: the title, description, location, date and time, how it repeats, the attendee limit, reminder times, colour, the host's time zone (so repeats stay at the same local time), and the Root user ID of whoever made it.
  • RSVPs: your Root user ID, which event and date it's for, your answer (going, maybe, can't go or waitlist) and when you gave it.
  • Announcement links: which chat message belongs to which event, so reactions on it count as RSVPs.

What It Doesn't Do

  • It doesn't read or store your messages. It only looks at reactions on its own event announcements.
  • It doesn't collect emails, IP addresses, locations or anything from outside Root.
  • It doesn't use analytics or tracking, and there are no ads.
  • It doesn't share or sell anything to anyone.
Who Can See It

RSVPs Are Public To Your Community

Like a sign-up sheet, the whole point is that people can see who's coming. Anyone in the community who can open the calendar can see each event's going, maybe, can't go and waitlist lists, with names and profile pictures. If an event is posted in a channel, that message lists the names of people going, maybe or on the waitlist, and updates when answers change.

While It's Working

What It Uses For A Moment

  • Your community nickname, to write names into announcement messages. It's held in memory for up to 10 minutes and not saved.
  • Your roles, to check whether you're allowed to host events.
  • Names and profile pictures in the calendar come straight from Root on your device when you open it.
Notifications

Who Gets Pinged

  • People who said going or maybe get reminders before an event, at the times the host picked.
  • If you're on a waitlist and a spot opens up, you get a notification that you're in.
  • Roles or members chosen by the community's admins get a notification when a new event is created.

Notifications are sent through Root, and you can control them in your Root settings.

Storage

Where It's Kept

Everything is stored in the app's own storage on Root's servers, separately for each community. It isn't sent anywhere else. Root's own privacy policy covers how Root handles your account, notifications and community data.

Deleting It

Getting It Removed

  • Your RSVP: click your answer again in the calendar, or take your reaction off the announcement, and it's deleted straight away.
  • Automatically: RSVPs are deleted 90 days after the event they're for.
  • Events: when a host deletes an event, its RSVPs go with it.
  • Everything: if a community uninstalls the app, it stops running there. If you want the leftover data wiped, or anything else removed, get in touch and I'll delete it.
Everything Else

Kids & Changes

The app follows Root's age rules and doesn't knowingly collect anything extra about anyone, kids included.

If the app ever starts saving something new, I'll update this page and the date at the top before that change goes out.

Questions?

If you have a question or want something deleted, get in touch and I'll sort it out.
